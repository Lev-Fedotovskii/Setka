3. Stop status from the notification, reopen Unstable, and confirm it stays stopped. Enable it again with an upcoming event, reboot, unlock, and check restoration without opening Unstable first.

Check portrait/landscape, a larger system font, keyboard entry and TalkBack if available. Dismiss a normal dialog after scrolling the page; the background should remain fixed and return to its previous position.

Do not report an unchecked step as passed. Browser and emulator evidence cannot replace physical battery-policy or accessibility checks.
